## 1. Finding a recipe

Go to **«Oppskrifter»** in the menu.

- **«Hva med…?»** suggests a dinner that suits the day (pizza on Fridays). **«Vis et annet forslag»** gives another one.
- **Search** in the search box: it looks in the titles, the descriptions and the ingredients («laks», «kokosmelk»).
- **The meal buttons** (Middag, Dessert …) are a quick filter. **«Filter»** (only the icon on a phone) opens every filter: main ingredient, time, difficulty, cuisine, «Passer til», diets, and with or without an ingredient. The numbers show how many recipes each choice gives.
- **Sort** by newest, A–Å or quickest.
- **The view** (big cards, cards or a list) is remembered on that device.
- **Your search and filters are in the address.** Copy the address to share a filtered list, and the back button keeps them.
- On the recipe page, **the labels** («Italiensk», «Rask») are links to every recipe with that label.

**Phone and computer** work the same way. On a phone, the filters open as a panel from the bottom.

## 2. Cooking from a recipe

- **«Porsjoner» (− 4 +)** changes every amount.
- **On a phone**, the ingredients come before the steps, and a bar under the header jumps between «Ingredienser», «Slik gjør du» and «Tips». **On a computer**, the ingredients are in a panel on the right that stays in view while you read the steps.
- **«Behold skjermen på»** keeps the phone from going dark while you cook.
- **Tap a step's number** when the step is done: it gets a ✓ and turns grey. Only kept while the page is open.
- **«Skriv ut»** prints the recipe with the ingredients, without the menu and buttons. **«Kopier lenke»** copies the address.

## 3. The pizza calculator

Go to **«Pizza-kalkulator»**. It always opens with the house recipe.

**The quick way** (most days):
1. **Choose the number of pizzas** with − and + in the black «Resultat» box. The grams change at once.
2. **Tap each line while you weigh** («MEL 451 g»): it's crossed out, so you can see what's already in the bowl.
3. **Set «Stekes kl.»** (when you want to eat) in «Fremgangsmåte». Every step gets its clock time, counted back from there.
4. **Turn on «Behold skjermen på»**, and follow the steps. Tap a step's number when it's done.

**«NÅ»** marks the step you should be on right now, according to the clock. It only follows the clock, not what you've actually done: if you started late, it's ahead of you. It assumes the baking is **today**, so for a long rise in the fridge (the day before), look at the clock times instead.

**Trying something else:** «Juster deigen» (the bar on a phone, or the left column on a computer) has the pizza type, the weight per ball, the water, the yeast, the rise and the extras. **«Husoppskriften»** at the top puts everything back.

**Phone or computer:** on a phone, the result comes first, then the method, then the settings, with a bar under the header to jump between them. On a computer, the settings are on the left and the result and method on the right.

## 4. The site as an app on your phone

The site can be added to the home screen, and then opens like an app: full screen, without Safari's address bar.

**iPhone:**
1. Open the site in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap **Del** (the square with an arrow) → **«Legg til på Hjem-skjerm»**.
3. Check that «Open as Web App» is on (the default in new iOS versions), and tap **«Legg til»**.

**Android:** in Chrome, the menu (⋮) → «Installer app» or «Legg til på startsiden» (the wording varies between versions).

**Good to know:**
- **The app always shows the newest version.** If it doesn't, swipe the app away completely and open it again.
- **The app has its own login,** separate from Safari: log in once inside the app (section 5).
- **It needs the internet,** like the site.
- **There's no «Del → Matnettside»** from the Photos app on an iPhone (iPhones don't allow it for web apps). Post photos from inside the app instead (section 6).

## 5. For the admin: logging in

- **«Logg inn»** is at the bottom of every page (in the footer), on a phone, a computer and in the app. Then **«Logg inn med Google»**. You come back to the page you were on.
- Only the admin's Google account can save anything. Anyone else can log in, but nothing they try to save gets through.
- **Logged in, a dark bar shows at the very top:** `Admin · + Ny oppskrift · Datasjekk · Logg ut`.
- You **stay logged in** on that device, also after closing it. On another device, or in the app, you log in there too.
- **Visitors see nothing of this**, apart from the «Logg inn» link.

## 6. For the admin: posting a recipe quickly

Made for the phone: post the dish while it's in front of you, and fill in the rest later.

1. **«+ Ny oppskrift»** in the admin bar.
2. Write **the title**. Add **a photo** if you have one (the phone offers the camera or the photo library).
3. **«Lagre»**: done. The recipe is online at once, marked «Uferdig» until it has ingredients. **Visitors don't see «Uferdig» recipes** in the overview, so it's fine to post half-done.

Want to fill it in right away? Choose **«Lagre og fyll ut resten»** instead: it saves and opens «Rediger».

## 7. For the admin: editing a recipe

Open the recipe, and press **«Rediger»** (next to «Skriv ut»). The recipe becomes a form.

- **«Mangler 3 av 9»** at the top lists what's still missing. Tap one to go straight to it. It's ticked off as you fill it in.
- **The bar at the top** jumps between «Bilde», «Detaljer», «Ingredienser» and «Steg».
- **The photo:** «Bytt bilde» for a new one. **Tap the photo** to choose what always stays in view when it's cut to fit the cards (a gold dot).
- **Ingredients:** one line each. Amounts like «2», «1,5», «½» or «1/2». **Enter** goes to the next line, or makes a new one. The name field suggests names you've used before: pick one, so «tomat» and «tomater» don't both happen. **«Lim inn liste»** turns a pasted list into lines («2 dl melk», «Løk, finhakket»; a line ending in «:» becomes a group heading).
- **Steps:** ↑ and ↓ move a step, «Fjern» removes it, «+ Legg til steg» adds one.
- **An empty field is removed** from the recipe. The address never changes, even with a new title.
- **«Lagre 3 endringer»** saves only what you changed, and says «Lagret ✓». **«Avbryt»** leaves without saving.
- **«Slett oppskrift»** at the very bottom deletes the recipe for good (it asks first).

**Nothing is lost by accident:**
- Leaving with unsaved changes asks first.
- What you type is kept on the device as a **draft**. After a crash or a closed tab, the button says **«Rediger (utkast)»**, and **«Gjenopprett»** brings it back (except a new photo).
- If the login has run out, **«Logg inn igjen»** logs you in without losing what you've written. Then press «Lagre» again.

**The Studio** (Sanity's own editing tool) still works as a backup. Edit a recipe in one place at a time: an unpublished draft in the Studio would overwrite what you saved on the site when it's published.

## 8. For the admin: tidying up with the Datasjekk

**«Datasjekk»** in the admin bar lists every recipe and what it's missing (photo, cuisine, time …), the most first.

- **«Rediger»** next to a recipe opens it in the editor.
- **Each missing thing is a link:** tap «Kjøkken» and the editor opens with the cursor in that field. Fill it in, «Lagre», and go back to the Datasjekk with the back button.
- At the bottom: **ingredient names that are almost the same** («tomat» / «tomater»). Make them the same, so the filters find both.

A good habit: post quickly during the week, and tidy up from the Datasjekk now and then.

## 9. When something doesn't work

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| The app shows an old version | Swipe the app away completely and open it again |
| Logged out in the app, but logged in in Safari | Normal: the app has its own login. Log in at the bottom of the page in the app |
| «Kunne ikke lagre: innloggingen har gått ut» | Press «Logg inn igjen», then «Lagre» again. What you wrote is kept |
| «Fikk ikke kontakt med serveren» | No internet. Your changes are kept as a draft; try again when you're online |
| A new recipe doesn't show for visitors | It's «Uferdig»: give it ingredients |
| The Google window doesn't open | Make sure pop-ups aren't blocked, and try «Logg inn» again |
| «Kunne ikke hente oppskriftene» | The recipes couldn't be fetched (the internet, or Sanity). Press «Prøv igjen» |
